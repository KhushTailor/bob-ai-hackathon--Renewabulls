Operational Challenge

The Microgrid Operator Dilemma

Acute Volatility & Intermittency

Solar dropouts and wind variability cause generation to swing by tens of MW within minutes, threatening system frequency.

Interconnection Bottlenecks

Import limits (180 MW cap) cannot be violated without emergency blackout risk or severe financial penalties.

Battery Reserve Degradation

Depleting battery energy below the physical 5% floor (4 MWh) causes irreversible degradation and leaves zero contingency buffer.

Disjoint Cognitive Overload

Controllers must cross-correlate disparate SCADA meters and spreadsheets in high-stress crisis windows.

Decision-Ready Capabilities

Five Core Decision-Support Features

  • 1. 72-Hour Demand Forecasting: 288 lookahead points at 15-min intervals with seasonal naive & Granite TTM.
  • 2. Renewable Performance Monitoring: Real-time solar & wind performance ratios (PR%), CF%, and curtailment alerts.
  • 3. 12-Rule Anomaly & Red-Flag Engine: Deterministic classification across frequency, voltage, ramps, and battery limits.
  • 4. 72-Hour What-If Simulator: Physics-bounded active power evaluation testing Battery Dispatch, Load Shift, and Import.
  • 5. AI Operator Brief: Structured synthesis answering 6 operational questions (What? Why? Risk? Action? Rationale? Consequences).

Physics Verification

True Energy Bounds & Feasibility

The what-if simulator enforces physical battery dynamics: $E = P \times \Delta t$

Verified 20 MW / 1h Dispatch

Initial: 50.2% SOC (40.16 MWh)
Discharge: 20 MW × 1h = 20 MWh
Remaining: 25.2% SOC (20.16 MWh)
Status: FEASIBLE (Preserves 20.2% reserve above 5% floor)

Infeasible Action Rejection

Over-import requests (>180 MW) or over-discharge requests (>36.16 MWh) are clamped, unmet power is reported, and feasibility is set to false.

Sustainability & Production Roadmap

  • Immediate Carbon & Asset Impact: Minimizes curtailment by routing excess solar/wind into batteries; extends utility BESS life via strict SOC floor protection.
